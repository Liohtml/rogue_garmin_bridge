## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-01

### Added
- Connect IQ watch app for fēnix 5 Plus/6/7/8/9/E and epix (Gen 2) / epix Pro.
- Bluetooth FTMS connection to the Rogue Echo Bike with auto-reconnect.
- Native indoor cycling activity with power, cadence, speed and distance in native FIT
  fields; lap and session averages, maxima, normalized power and total work.
- Developer fields for console calories, kcal/h, console heart rate, MET and resistance.
- English and German UI.
- Login-free SDK setup, build script, CI and release workflow.
